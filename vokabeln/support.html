Frequently asked questions

Why does a word come back days later?
Every “Got it” moves a word further into the future (1 → 3 → 7 → 16 → 35 → 90 days). “Not yet” brings it back tomorrow. A few minutes every day works best.

When does a word count as “learned”?
When you got it right on two different days.

How do I sync my iPhone, iPad and Mac?
Automatically through iCloud, if all devices use the same Apple Account and iCloud is on. Coming from the web version? There: Settings → “Send progress”. In the app: Settings → “Load progress file”.

There's no sound.
Check the speaker button at the top and your device's silent switch. For a better voice: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Portuguese (Portugal).

Where are new words?
The word list is updated online; the app downloads new words by itself when you're online.

Privacy Policy

Português Vocab (iPhone, iPad and Mac app, and the web version) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: the app does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your learning progress belongs to you and stays on your devices.

Your learning progress

Which words you have practised and when they are due again is saved on your device. In the iPhone/iPad/Mac app it is also synced through your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage), so your devices stay in step. The developer has no access to your iCloud data. You can switch iCloud off for the app in your device settings, and “Reset progress” in the app deletes it.

In the web version, progress is stored only in your browser (local storage) on that device.

If you use “Send progress file”, a small file with your progress is created and shared only with the person or place you choose.

Word list downloads

When you are online, the app checks this website (hosted by GitHub Pages) for an updated word list. Like any web server, GitHub may briefly log technical data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement. No information about you or your progress is sent.

Pronunciation

Words are spoken by your device's built-in text-to-speech voices. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Links to other websites

The “Infopedia” buttons open the Infopedia dictionary website in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions? See the support page.
